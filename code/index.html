THE PROJECT

HEAL is an interactive digital exhibition created for RMIT University’s COMM2754 — Digital Media Specialisation 1.

Four generative artworks trace the consequences of traditional energy, the accumulation of pollution, and a transition toward cleaner energy and environmental recovery. Your actions connect the four chapters.

SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT GOAL

AFFORDABLE AND
CLEAN ENERGY

SDG 7 calls for energy that people can afford and rely on, while increasing renewable energy and improving efficiency. HEAL explores the environmental consequences of energy choices and the possibility of a collective transition.

In ADAPT, solar and wind work together as a connected system. LIVEN imagines the life that cleaner energy can support. Recovery is our artistic narrative; care for communities and ecosystems continues beyond the artwork.
