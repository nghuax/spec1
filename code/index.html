THE PROJECT

HEAL is an interactive digital exhibition created for RMIT University’s COMM2754 — Digital Media Specialisation 1.

Four generative artworks trace the consequences of traditional energy, the accumulation of pollution, and a transition toward cleaner energy and environmental recovery. Your actions connect the four chapters.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Energy supports everyday life. SDG 7 asks whether everyone can access energy that is affordable, reliable and sustainable.

ENERGY + ENVIRONMENT

Burning fossil fuels releases greenhouse gases. HARM and EXHAUST make environmental pressure visible through your interactions.

WHY RENEWABLE ENERGY

Sun and wind are replenished naturally. Generating energy from renewables produces far fewer emissions than burning fossil fuels. ADAPT and LIVEN explore that possibility through reconnection and renewal.

SDG 7 CONNECTION

Targets 7.1–7.3 connect energy access, renewable energy and efficiency. HEAL’s recovery is an artistic interpretation: real change also depends on infrastructure, community choices and care for ecosystems.
