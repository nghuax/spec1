ADAPTS — Individual Reflection and Peer Evaluation
Nguyen Gia Toan Phu Nghia — S4099019

Assignment 2 changed my project from a predictable renewable-energy landscape into a stronger generative system. My early version communicated SDG 7 through familiar solar panels and turbines, but the final result could be understood before the animation began. Class feedback made me focus on the audience’s perspective: typography needed to stay readable, interactions needed clear cues, and decorative fragments could not compete with the message. I therefore developed two contrasting states. The damaged state uses grey depth, broken habitats and unstable movement; recovery assembles the same primitives into a large shared land and a colourful network. Randomized land, habitats and fragments now produce a fresh composition on every reset while consistent constraints protect the interface. I also improved three A1 sounds and recorded paper, water and keyboard sources for A2, connecting wind, assembly and recovery to meaningful moments rather than using sound as decoration.

Teamwork improved through comparison and reduction. Luong Duc Hung, Nguyen Tran Phuc Duong and Ngo Dac Phu shared their stage work and feedback, which helped us align Stack Sans Notch typography, the blue–purple–orange–lime palette, flat primitive assets and the HARM–EXHAUST–ADAPT–LIVEN sequence. My main contribution was the ADAPT concept, p5-2026-09-22-v01.js artwork, animation, optional SUN/WIND interaction, sound system, Figma-aligned interface, testing and documentation. The group process was constructive because each member kept a distinct part of the story while agreeing on the strongest common visual rules.
