WHY RENEWABLE ENERGY

Renewable electricity produces far fewer emissions than fossil fuels. ADAPT uses recovery as an artistic expression of reducing environmental pressure.

SDG 7 CONNECTION

Target 7.2 calls for a greater share of renewable energy. In the real world, reliable networks and access matter alongside the energy source.
